This closure responds to a discussion of the applicability of predictors for clear-water and live-bed scour at circular piers, particularly the limits of laboratory calibration for sediment coarseness. Using a selected set of 298 field observations, the authors compare the distributions of pier slenderness, flow intensity, sediment coarseness, sediment gradation, and relative scour depth with those of the laboratory dataset. The comparison indicates that laboratory data represent field ranges reasonably well for pier slenderness and flow intensity, but cover only part of the field variability in sediment coarseness and gradation. The closure highlights the need for further research and more representative measurements to improve the applicability of scour prediction formulas under field conditions.
